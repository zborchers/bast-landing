Most of us were never taught to actually look for why our energy is gone — we just manage the symptoms. More coffee. More sleep that doesn't fix it. A supplement stack that helps a little and stops. Fatigue, chronic symptoms, and burnout aren't usually a mystery. They're the result of your energy being diverted somewhere specific, for long enough that your body started showing the cost.

Your body can't lie about this the way your mind can talk itself out of noticing.
