You're more energy than matter

We're living through an evolutionary shift, one that asks us to rethink what we've long assumed about the body. For generations, health has been treated as a purely physical matter, something to be measured, medicated, and managed. But your body isn't only physical. It exists across many planes at once: physical, yes, but also energetic, emotional, mental, relational, and spiritual, all woven together rather than separated into parts. Your body isn't separate from your energy. It's your energy, held together in a shape solid enough for you to feel.

Physical health follows energetic health. It doesn't lead it.

Most health advice never makes it past the physical layer, and when it tries, it tends to splinter into disconnected specialties rather than gather into one coherent picture. Our current model treats the symptom in front of it without asking the question underneath every symptom: how much energy do you actually have, and where has it been going?

Energy is meant to move, to transform, to create. When it can't, it builds up as density, and that density eventually surfaces in the body, whether as a symptom, a diagnosis, or an ache that won't resolve. This is why physical health and energetic health aren't two different things, only two different angles on the same one. Nothing appears in the body without first appearing in the energy beneath it. The energetic always arrives first. The physical follows.

The reframe

Most of us give our energy to things that never give it back

Scrolling. Replaying an old argument. News built to scare you. A friendship that leaves you a little more tired every time you hang up. We call this normal because it's familiar, not because it's good for us.

The fix: spend your energy on what actually gives it back. Real food. Water. Sleep. Letting go of what you can't change. Moving your body somewhere like a sauna or LiveO2 session, built to clear you out, not just burn calories. Being around people who leave you fuller than they found you.

Real love expands you. Whatever isn't real will contract you — even when it calls itself love.

That's what "voltage" means here. Not how much you're doing. How much you're holding, and how much you give off.
