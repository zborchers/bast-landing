How I find your root cause

Every symptom has an energetic intelligence behind it. I understand how to read the body and interpret the energetic intelligence that's beneath the physical. Here's how I do it:

Left side symptoms relate to receiving, inner emotional life, and what's happening internally. Right side symptoms connect to doing, performance, and external world pressures.
The body has an energetic architecture, often referred to as the chakras, that runs parallel to its physical one. The chakras are energetic centers of intelligence that govern specific body parts, life domains, and powers/energy. This is the map I base my reading on. Where energy flows freely, life opens. Where it's blocked or diverted, the body speaks.
The physical and the energetic are never in opposition. Every symptom has both a biological and an energetic dimension operating simultaneously. I never ask you to choose between medicine and root cause — both are always true at the same time.
Some of what drains you isn't yours alone. Patterns, fears, and energy drains can move through family lines across generations. I also read those patterns and help you understand what's actually running in the background.
The people you keep saying yes to, the conflicts that repeat, the roles you keep playing — none of these are accidents. They're archetypal patterns, and I use these as context to understand your energetic health.
