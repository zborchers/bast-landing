How it works

How we find your root cause

Every symptom is energy that's stuck somewhere specific — diverted, blocked, or drained for long enough that your body started showing the cost. Finding the root cause means finding exactly where that's happening, and why. This isn't generic wellness advice. It's a specific framework for reading your body, your patterns, and your life as one connected system, then tracing the drain back to its actual source.

Left side symptoms relate to receiving, inner emotional life, and what's happening internally. Right side symptoms connect to doing, performance, and external world pressures.
Every symptom lives somewhere, and that location is meaningful. Each region of the body corresponds to an energy center governing a specific life domain. Where something hurts — or where your energy consistently drops — tells you which part of your life the drain is coming from.
The physical and the energetic are never in opposition. Every symptom has both a biological and an energetic dimension operating simultaneously. The framework never asks you to choose between medicine and root cause — both are always true at the same time.
Some of what drains you isn't yours alone. Patterns, fears, and energy drains can move through family lines across generations. The framework reads those patterns and helps you understand what's actually been running in the background.
Every drain has a reason it's still running — some part of you is being protected by staying exactly as you are. Naming that honestly, without judgment, is usually the first real step toward getting the energy back.
The people you keep saying yes to, the conflicts that repeat, the roles you keep playing — these aren't accidents. The framework reads relational patterns as places energy is consistently being diverted, showing you what those relationships are actually costing you.
The body has an energetic architecture that runs parallel to its physical one — distinct centers, each governing a specific life domain, each holding a specific kind of power. This is the map the framework reads from. Where energy flows freely, life opens. Where it's blocked or diverted, the body speaks.
